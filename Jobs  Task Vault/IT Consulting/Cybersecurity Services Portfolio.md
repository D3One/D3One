### **1. Offensive Security & Penetration Testing**

#### **1.1 Web Application Penetration Testing**

**Description:**
This service, often described as an ethical hacking exercise, assesses the security posture of your web applications by simulating real-world attacks. The goal is to identify and exploit vulnerabilities before malicious actors can do so.

Our assessment is conducted using a combination of automated security scanners and manual testing techniques, following internationally recognized methodologies such as the **OWASP Top 10**, **OSSTMM**, and best practices from standards like **PCI DSS**.

**Key Testing Activities:**
*   Vulnerability analysis of server-side components (back-end) and web server software frameworks.
*   Testing for critical flaws like Remote Code Execution (RCE), buffer overflows, and SQL Injection (SQLi).
*   Attempts to bypass authentication and authorization mechanisms.
*   Checks for Cross-Site Scripting (XSS), Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF), and open redirect vulnerabilities.
*   Security analysis of input forms (login, search, registration).
*   Race condition attacks and credential brute-forcing attempts.

**Why It's Relevant:**
*   **Accessible Attack Tools:** A wide array of hacking tools and tutorials are available online, enabling even low-skilled "script kiddies" to cause significant damage.
*   **Black-Market Services:** Hacking and DDoS attacks can be purchased anonymously, posing risks from unfair competition or targeted revenge.
*   **Constant Vulnerability Emergence:** Websites built on common frameworks and Content Management Systems (CMS) require regular patching. A site that was secure yesterday may be vulnerable today due to newly discovered flaws.
*   **GDPR & Data Breach Risks:** A compromised website can lead to the theft of personal data, potentially resulting in severe fines under GDPR (up to 4% of global annual turnover or €20 million).

**Ideal For:**
*   E-commerce platforms and online marketplaces handling transactions and personal data.
*   Online service providers (hotel bookings, ticket sales, taxi services).
*   Any business reliant on its website for operations and customer service.

#### **1.2 IT Infrastructure Penetration Testing**

**Description:**
This service is a "technical audit" that simulates the actions of a determined attacker attempting to breach your corporate network perimeter. It provides unambiguous answers to critical security questions.

**Key Objectives:**
*   Determine the resilience of your entire IT infrastructure (servers, CRM/ERP systems, internal portals, databases).
*   Estimate the time a skilled attacker would need to penetrate your network and exfiltrate or destroy critical data.
*   Identify which security controls failed and provide actionable recommendations for improvement.

**Scope of Testing:**
*   Servers running Windows Server (including Active Directory) and Linux.
*   Database Management Systems (MS SQL Server, Oracle, MySQL).
*   Business applications (e.g., 1C, MS Dynamics).
*   Network equipment (switches, VoIP systems, video conferencing, PBX).

**Indicators of a Successful Breach:**
*   Gaining administrative access to OS, DBMS, or network devices.
*   Bypassing or disabling security software (antivirus, firewalls).
*   Ability to copy, modify, or delete sensitive financial or operational data.
*   Causing denial-of-service (shutdown) for critical systems.

**Why It's Relevant:**
This is the most effective way to obtain undeniable evidence of your security posture, far surpassing the theoretical assurance of a compliance-only audit.

**Ideal For:**
*   Financial institutions, payment gateways, and brokerages.
*   Any organization serious about validating its cyber defenses.

### **2. Compliance & Security Audits**

#### **2.1 IT General Controls (ITGC) & Compliance Audit**

**Description:**
This audit provides objective, verified information on the state of your IT infrastructure and its security, focusing on two key areas:
1.  Formal compliance with standards (FSTEC Orders #17, 21, 31; Bank of Russia standards; SOX 404; PCI DSS; NIST-800).
2.  The adequacy of organizational and technical measures for secure operation (e.g., access rights management, privileged accounts, change management, backups).

**Why It's Relevant:**
*   Provides C-level management and business owners with a reliable overview of IT readiness and security.
*   A mandatory component of financial audits for entities subject to the **Sarbanes-Oxley Act (SOX)**.

**Key Audit Areas (based on PCAOB/SEC standards):**
*   Data backup and recovery procedures.
*   Access control and administrative account management.
*   Segregation of Duties (SoD).
*   Separation of IT environments (Dev, Staging, Prod).
*   Change management and patch management policies.
*   Logging, monitoring, and incident response.

**Methodology:**
*   Interviews with client personnel (IT admins, etc.).
*   Completion of specialized questionnaires.
*   Request and review of relevant documentation (IT policies, procedures).
*   Manual and automated testing of controls.

**Ideal For:**
*   Companies under SOX regulation.
*   Organizations undergoing mergers or acquisitions (Due Diligence).
*   Any business needing a rapid, standards-based assessment of its IT environment.

#### **2.2 Technical Security Hardening Audit**

**Description:**
This audit provides a deep technical assessment of the security configuration ("hardening state") of specific IT systems, such as operating systems, databases, and network equipment.

**Why It's Relevant:**
It reveals the actual technical security posture of systems, providing a level of detail not achievable through penetration tests or ITGC audits.

**Methodology:**
*   Specialized software is used to take a "snapshot" of a system's current security configuration.
*   This snapshot is compared against an ideal "baseline" model built from vendor recommendations, best practices (e.g., CIS Benchmarks), and expert experience.
*   Specific tools are used for each type of IT system.

**Key Differentiator:**
Recommendations focus on optimizing built-in security features and configurations **without necessarily requiring the purchase of additional commercial security products.**

**Ideal For:**
*   Organizations focused on practical security improvements over formal compliance documentation.

### **3. Regulatory Compliance & Data Protection**

#### **3.1 Personal Data Protection Compliance (Russian Federal Law 152-FZ)**

**Description:**
A comprehensive "turnkey" service to ensure full compliance with Russian data protection laws, including Federal Law 152-FZ "On Personal Data," Government Regulation P-1119, and FSTEC Order #21.

**Scope of Works:**
*   **Assessment & Scoping:** Data collection, identification of Personal Data Information Systems (ISPDn), and definition of the security perimeter.
*   **Classification:** Formal classification of ISPDn, formation of an expert commission, and official documentation.
*   **Threat Modeling:** Development of a custom threat model (attacker profiles, relevant threats, compensating controls).
*   **Policy Development:** Creation of all required internal documentation (Data Protection Regulation, administrator/user instructions, log forms, agreements).
*   **Technical Configuration (Optional):** Hardening of built-in security mechanisms in OS, DBMS, and network services.
*   **Solution Selection & Implementation (Optional):** Recommendation, installation, and configuration of additional security tools tailored to the ISPDn protection level.
*   **Training & Support (Optional):** Staff training and ongoing advisory support.

**Why It's Relevant:**
Compliance with Federal Law 152-FZ is mandatory for all data operators processing personal data of Russian citizens.

**Ideal For:**
*   All organizations falling under the jurisdiction of Russian data protection laws.
